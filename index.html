Cross-platform gameplay trends

Five patterns that show up across the three charts. The facts come from the game pages and press coverage; the takeaways are our reading.

Strongest pattern

"Steal it, earn from it" still rules Roblox - with a new twist

Steal An Egg stays number one at roughly 527K concurrent. Newcomer Throw Food For Animals (about 187K, straight in at four) swaps stealing for throw food to attract animals, pen them for income, train Throw Power, unlock farther zones. The shared core: collect, place for passive income, upgrade to open the next gate. The theme is freely swappable.

Physics sandbox

Ragdoll physics finds a new audience

Hyper Realistic Ragdoll (about 77K) is a new top-ten entry built around exaggerated, almost-realistic body motion that spreads through short clips and memes. A low-effort physics toy plus an imitation meme is a repeatable viral pattern.

Subtraction design

Realistic tactical FPS: less HUD, more immersion

Bodycam entered Steam's top sellers at number three. No crosshair, HUD or ammo counter: players read the fight through spatial audio and callouts. The lesson for map design: make information gathering the gameplay, not just another overlay.

Hero competition

Hero abilities plus team play stay on top

Counter-Strike 2 leads, Marvel Rivals is second and Apex Legends is seventh. Three competitive shooters on one chart show the staying power of distinct roles and teamwork.

Low-pressure social

Cozy collecting and freeform RP remain huge

Disney Dreamlight Valley joins the chart while Brookhaven RP and Adopt Me! sit second and third on Roblox. Open sandboxes, character collecting and UGC decorating anchor the most stable casual audience.
