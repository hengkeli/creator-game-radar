Cross-platform gameplay trends

Five patterns that show up across the three charts. The facts come from the game pages and press coverage; the takeaways are our reading.

Strongest pattern

"Steal it, carry it home, earn from it" dominates Roblox

Steal An Egg is the most-played experience on Roblox and the only one above one million concurrent players in this snapshot. Steal a Brainrot is also in the top 10. Both share one loop: grab something valuable, make it back to base, earn passive income from it, then raid other players. The tension lives in the run home.

Co-op fear

Co-op survival horror keeps landing

99 Nights in the Forest and Dandy's World on Roblox, Dead by Daylight on Steam, and XANNIBAN on GameJolt all pair a threat with a clear objective and teammates.

Low-barrier social

Voting and roleplay still scale

Dress To Impress wins on player voting, not combat. Murder Mystery 2 and Adopt Me! show how simple social rules can last for years.

Indie breakout

A silly premise with a real system underneath

Moon Sweepers (clean the entire moon, starting with a toothbrush) hid a logistics and power-grid game behind the joke and peaked around 36,000 concurrent players on Steam within days of launch, from a two-person team. SuperWEIRD on GameJolt follows the same idea with idle automation.

Readable action

Clear combat signals sell

Phantom Blade Zero uses colour-coded attacks (blue to parry, red to dodge) and weapon swapping to keep fast fights legible.
